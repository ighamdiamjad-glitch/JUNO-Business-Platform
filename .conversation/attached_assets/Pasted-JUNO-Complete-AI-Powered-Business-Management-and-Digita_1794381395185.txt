Official Name: JUNO
Pronunciation: JOO-noh
Official Slogan: From Imagination to Business.

PROJECT OVERVIEW

I want to build a complete, independent, professional AI-powered platform called JUNO.

JUNO must not be merely a landing page, a visual prototype, a basic chatbot, or a collection of non-functional interface elements.

I want a fully developed, scalable, independent application with its own user interface, dashboard, backend, database, artificial intelligence capabilities, business management tools, marketing tools, automation capabilities, and integrations with external services.

JUNO should function as an intelligent business consultant, business intelligence assistant, and digital marketing manager.

The platform must be designed for long-term development, expansion, and real-world use.

Critical priority: Digital marketing is one of the most important core capabilities of JUNO. It must be developed as a comprehensive marketing management and execution system, not just a content-writing assistant.

10. ACTUAL MARKETING EXECUTION AND AUTOMATION

I do not want JUNO to stop at planning and content generation.

I want it to be capable of executing authorized marketing tasks through official platform APIs and supported integrations.

Required execution workflow

1. JUNO researches and develops a marketing idea.
2. JUNO creates the required content and campaign materials.
3. JUNO presents the materials to the authorized user for review.
4. JUNO requests explicit approval for the specific action.
5. After approval, JUNO executes the authorized operation through the connected official service.
6. JUNO records the operation and its result.
7. JUNO monitors available performance data.
8. JUNO generates reports and improvement recommendations.

Mandatory approval and safety requirements

* Never publish content without explicit approval.
* Never schedule content without the required approval.
* Never send marketing messages without explicit authorization.
* Never launch advertising campaigns without explicit approval.
* Never spend advertising money without explicit approval.
* Approval for one post does not authorize other posts.
* Approval for one campaign does not authorize other campaigns.
* Significant changes to content, audience, timing, or budget require renewed approval.
* The authorized user must be able to stop pending tasks.
* The authorized user must be able to revoke external integrations and permissions.
* Maintain a complete audit log of approvals and executed actions.
* Never claim that an operation was completed unless the connected service confirms its success.

Use official APIs and comply with each platform’s permissions, policies, and technical restrictions.

Do not assume that every platform supports every publishing or advertising operation.

2. Do not use fake data as if it were real business data.
3. Do not create non-functional buttons.
4. Do not claim that a feature is complete before its backend and functionality have been implemented and tested.
5. Do not invent external API connections.
6. Do not claim that external publishing or advertising has succeeded without confirmation from the connected service.
7. Do not publish content or spend money without explicit authorization.
8. Do not expose sensitive credentials.
9. Do not remove or simplify essential requirements.
10. Keep the code organized, modular, maintainable, and scalable.
11. Use reusable components.
12. Document setup requirements and external dependencies.
13. Clearly explain which features are implemented, which are partially implemented, and which still require external integrations.
14. Test each development phase before moving to the next one.
15. Do not move to the next phase until the current phase has been reviewed and tested.
16. Prioritize the digital marketing system as one of JUNO’s most important capabilities.
17. Make the application independent and capable of future expansion.
